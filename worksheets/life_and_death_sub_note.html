Sub plan

(Life & Death week)

Mr. Lewis

Thank you for covering my class! I'm at an art conference all week. Students finish their current project, then start a short one called Life & Death. They'll finish at different times, and that's fine. Please leave 5 minutes for cleanup every period. Bell schedules and rules are in my sub reference guide.

01The week
  • Until they finish: students keep working on their current contour project. Finished work goes in .
  • When they finish: they take a Life & Death sheet from .
  • Around midweek, once most of the class is ready, read the script below.
  • Pace: day 1 list + sketches, days 2–3 the final on the back.
02Read this out loud
"This is a 4-point wild card. The back of a truck has two doors. On the left door, draw something alive, new or whole, and make it look real. On the right door, show that same thing destroyed or transformed. Clever, not gory: no weapons aimed at people or animals, no blood, no self-harm, no real people. Start with your list of 20, then six quick sketches, then draw your best one big on the back."
  • Examples: the book on my desk, pages 112 and 113. Pass it around or put it under the document camera.
03If a drawing crosses the line
  • Gory or violent: no need to argue. Ask them to pick another idea from their list. If they won't, put a sticky note on it and leave it on my desk.
  • Anything that looks like a student might hurt themselves or someone else: don't wait for me. Follow the school's protocol and contact the counselor or front office that period.
04Collect
  • Last day of the week: collect every Life & Death sheet, finished or not, and put them in . I'd rather grade an unfinished one than lose it.
  • Done early? They can draw a second truck on blank paper for bonus points.
