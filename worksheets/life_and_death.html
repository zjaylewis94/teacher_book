Life & Death

(the truck problem)

Name
Period

Picking the wrong side to pass a semi truck can be life or death. Your job: turn the truck's two back doors into a two-part story.

01The problem
Left door = LifeDraw one object, animal, plant or food. Make it accurate: real shape, real shading. It should look alive, new or whole.
Right door = DeathShow the same thing destroyed or transformed. Someone walking by should get it in 3 seconds.
Taken, so think of your own: candle → wax puddle · ice cream → sidewalk drip · phone at 100% → 1% · tree → stump · sandcastle → wave.
02The rules
  • The same subject on both doors.
  • Fill each door edge to edge.
  • Pencil first, then color or ink.
Clever, not gory. Make people smile or think. No weapons aimed at people or animals, no blood, no self-harm, no real people.
03List 20 things that can live or die. Objects count too!
04Sketch 6 your best ideas, quick, in pencil. Circle the one that reads fastest.
Wild Card · Sub week · 4 points
05Final your best sketch, big. Pencil first, then color or ink. Done early? Do a second truck for bonus.
