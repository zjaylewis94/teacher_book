Life & Death

(the truck problem)

No color =
not done!

Picking the wrong side to pass a semi truck can be life or death. Your job: turn the truck's two back doors into a two-part story.

01The problem
Left door = LifeDraw one object, animal, plant or food. Make it accurate: real shape, real shading. It looks alive, new or whole.
Right door = DeathShow the same thing destroyed or transformed. A stranger should get it in 3 seconds.
Taken: these two, a phone at 1%, a tree stump, a sandcastle. Think of your own!
02The rules
  • The same subject on both doors.
  • Fill each door edge to edge, background too.
  • Pencil first, then color it. No color = not done.
Clever, not gory. Make people smile or think. No weapons aimed at people or animals, no blood, no self-harm, no real people.
03How to work everything goes on the back
1List 5things that can live or die. Objects count!
2Pick 1the one that reads fastest. Circle it.
3Draw itbig on the truck, in pencil
4Color itboth doors, edge to edge. Then it's done.
04How it's graded 4 pts · done early? Do a 2nd truck for bonus
1 ptPlanning: 5 ideas listed, best one circled.
1 ptLife door: accurate shape, with real shading.
1 ptDeath door: the same subject, and it reads in 3 seconds.
1 ptColored: both doors fully colored, edge to edge.
