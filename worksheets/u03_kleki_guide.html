Kleki

(cheat sheet)

Kleki is the free paint app we use on the Chromebooks. Digital coloring works like a coloring book printed on clear plastic: the line art sits on top, and your color goes on its own layer underneath, so the lines stay clean.

01Get the line art
  1. 1Open Google Classroom and go to the class Drive folder. Pick one image to color.
  2. 2Open the image and hit Download. It lands in your Chromebook's Files → Downloads.
  3. Only use images from the class folder. They are made see-through so color can show from underneath. Images from a Google search won't work the same way.
02Bring it into Kleki
  1. 3Go to kleki.com. Click the File tab, then Import. Choose your image from Downloads and open it.
  2. 4First pop-up: click As Layer. (As Image would wipe your canvas.)
  3. 5Second pop-up: click Fit, then Center, then Ok.
03Make the color layer
  1. 6You now have two layers: the white background and your line art. Open the Layers tab and click + for a new layer. It shows up at the very top.
  2. 7Drag the new layer down so it sits under the line art and above the background.
  3. 8Optional: click Rename and pick Colors, so you never mix the layers up.
Golden rule: Color only on the color layer. Before every stroke, look at the Layers tab and check that the color layer is the one highlighted. If you paint on the line art layer, your color will cover the lines.

05Paint bucket tips
  1. ABe on the color layer, then click inside a shape to fill it.
  2. BSet Sample to All. That lets the bucket see the line art and stop at the lines, even though the lines live on another layer.
  3. CColor leaked everywhere? The lines have a gap. Undo, then paint that shape in with the brush.
06Save your work

Kleki does not save by itself. Close the tab and your work is gone. Save before the bell: File tab → pick a File Format → Save. It goes to Downloads.

PNGThe finished picture. One flat image, no layers. This is the one you turn in.
PSDThe working file. Keeps every layer, so you can keep painting tomorrow.

Opening a PSD again: File → Import → choose the PSD → click As Image and leave Flatten image unchecked. (As Layer squashes it into one layer.)

07Something looks wrong?
My color is covering the lines.You're on the line art layer, or the color layer is above it. Undo, then drag the color layer under.
I'm painting but nothing shows.The color layer slid under the white background. Drag it above the background.
The bucket filled the whole page.Undo. Check Sample is on All, and look for a gap in the lines.
A shortcut isn't working.Click the canvas once, then try again. Typing in a box turns shortcuts off.
