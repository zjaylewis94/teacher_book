Pen Shading

(techniques)

Value is how light or dark something is. A pen can't make gray, so you build value with marks: more marks, closer together = darker. White paper is your lightest value. Match the five values below.

Value key
Watch out
Hairy hatchinglines that wander in every direction
Comma dotsdragging the pen instead of tapping
Blob scribblescribbling so tight it turns into a black blob
