Pen Shading

(4 techniques)

Pencil gets darker when you press harder. Ink doesn't. In ink, value comes from how many marks you make and how close together they are: more marks = darker. Light comes from the top left, so the right sides and the cast shadows are your darkest darks.

Contour lines wrap around a form, like the path an ant would walk across it (light blue below). Point your marks along these curves and the form turns 3D. Flat surfaces get straight lines, curved surfaces get curved lines.
