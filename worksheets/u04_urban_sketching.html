Insight · Art in the Park · Intro to Urban Sketching
1Sit still long enough to observe.Watch for 2 whole minutes before your first line.
2Look for a "story moment."What's happening? That's your subject.
3Start w/ large shapes! No detail.Story moment first: big, in the middle. Then build out.
4Draw people as gestures, not portraits.Bean body, stick limbs. Show what they're doing.
5Use line weight to fake depth.Close = thick + dark.
Far = thin + light.
6Break perspective rules(strategically!) Lean it or bend it if it tells the story.
7Leave stuff out.Not every brick or leaf. Let the edges fade away.
8Don't erase!Pen from line one. No pencil = nothing to erase.
9Treat mistakes like they belong.Wrong line? Draw the right one next to it.
10Embrace speed.Fast lines look alive. People move, so you move.
11Add notes or tiny text.The time, the place, what you heard.
12Limited color pops.Ink first. Then 2–3 colors, only where it matters.
Ink, wait, paint. Microns are waterproof once they dry. Give the ink a minute, then add your color pops with watercolor.

Insight · Art in the Park · Intro to Urban Sketching
Don't draw the whole park! Find the one thing that's happening, draw it big in the middle of your page, then let the world grow out around it.
Look around for one of these... (or find your own)
Someone waiting
Someone working
Someone selling
A dog on a mission
Something being made
About to happen...
Two people talking
A kid + a snack
Size check: your story moment should be about the size of your fist on the page. Smaller than that? Start over bigger.
1st the story moment.Big, in the middle. Thick lines.
2nd big shapes.What's touching it? Add that next.
3rd the setting.Thin lines going out. Edges fade.
4th pops + notes.2–3 colors, tiny text, sign it!
Notice how step 1 has nothing else yet. That's on purpose. Get the moment right and the rest is easy.
How it's graded (8 pts · 2 = yes · 1 = partly · 0 = missing)
Story moment: one clear subject, big, near the middle. Center out: a setting built around it, edges fade. Ink: pen only, thick close + thin far, gesture people. Finish: 2–3 color pops, notes or tiny text, signed.
Keep sketching! Fill a page a week. Try your lunch table, your street from the curb, or a waiting room (everyone sits still. Free models!). Same rules: find the moment, start big, build out.
