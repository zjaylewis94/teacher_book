Da Rules

(of urban sketching)

Micron only.
No pencil!
1.Sit still long enough to observeWatch for 2 minutes before your first line.
2.Look for a "story moment"What's happening? That's your subject.
3.Start w/ large shapes! No detailStory moment first: big, in the middle. Build out.
4.Draw people as gestures, not portraitsBean body, stick limbs. Show the action.
5.Use line weight to fake depthClose = thick + dark. Far = thin.
6.Break perspective rules(strategically) Lean it or bend it if it tells the story.
7.Leave stuff outNot every brick or leaf. Let the edges fade away.
8.Don't erase!Pen from line one. Nothing to erase.
9.Treat mistakes like they belongWrong line? Draw the right one next to it.
10.Embrace speedFast lines look alive. People move, so you move.
11.Add notes or tiny textThe time, the place, what you heard.
12.Limited color popsInk first. 2–3 colors, only where it matters.
Ink, wait, paint. Microns are waterproof once they dry. Give the ink a minute, then add your color pops with watercolor. The numbers in the sketch show where each rule happens.

Center Out

(how to build a sketch)

Don't draw the whole park. Find the one thing that's happening, draw it big in the middle of your page, then let the world grow out around it.

01Find the story moment
  • Someone waiting in line
  • Someone working or selling
  • An animal doing its thing
  • Something being made
  • Something about to happen
  • Two people talking
Size check: your story moment should be about the size of your fist on the page. Smaller than that? Start over bigger.
02Build it from the center out
1The moment

Big, in the middle. Thick lines.

2Big shapes

What's touching it? Add that next.

3The setting

Thin lines going out. Let the edges fade.

4Pops + notes

2–3 colors, tiny text, sign it.

04How it's graded 8 pts · 2 = yes · 1 = partly · 0 = missing
2 ptsStory moment: one clear subject, drawn big, near the middle.
2 ptsCenter out: a setting built around it, with edges that fade out.
2 ptsInk: pen only, thick close and thin far, people as gestures.
2 ptsFinish: 2–3 color pops, notes or tiny text, and signed.
