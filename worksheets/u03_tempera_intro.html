Tempera

(intro worksheet)

Tempera is the classic poster paint: thick, bright and flat. It mixes with water, dries in minutes and covers what's underneath, so you can paint light over dark. It's the best paint for learning to mix color, because what you mix is what you get.

03Handling paint
Load 1/3 of the brushDip the tip, wipe the extra on the rim.
Rinse, then blotEvery time you change colors.
Dark into lightAdd the dark color a dab at a time.
Don't park the brushLeft in water, the bristles bend for good.
Golden rule: A little dark goes a long way. Start with the light color and add the dark one a dab at a time. You can always add more. You can't take it back out.
04Color words
← TINTS (color + white)HUESHADES (color + black) →
Hue: the pure color, straight from the bottle.
Value: how light or dark a color is.
Tint: a hue with white added. Lighter.
Shade: a hue with black added. Darker.
Tone: a hue with gray added. Duller.
Opaque: you can't see through it.
Value scale: even steps from light to dark.
Matte: a flat finish with no shine.

Tempera warm-ups

Complete all five boxes with tempera. This isn't about making something pretty. It's about finding out what the paint does. Do box 2A first, so it's dry by the end.

1Brush marks

One color, three marks. Find out how many kinds of line one brush can make.

B · flat brush on its edge
C · round brush: a line, then dots
2Cover-up test start now, finish last

A: fill the box with a dark color and let it dry. B: at the very end, paint a light color over the right half of A.

3Water test
A · paint straight from the palette
B · the same paint + lots of water
Which one hides the paper?  Which one is streaky?
4Value scale: tints to shades

Pick one color and paint it pure in the middle box. Going left, add a little more white to each box. Going right, add a little more black. Aim for even jumps, like stairs.

← TINTSHUESHADES →
almost whitea little whitepure colora little blackalmost black
Mixing tip: For tints, start with white and add color. For shades, start with color and add a tiny dab of black.
5Smooth blend

Same color. Blend from white, to pure color, to black with no steps showing. Work fast, while the paint is wet.

Quick fact: Tempera is one of the oldest paints there is. For centuries, painters mixed their color with egg yolk to make it stick. The school kind swaps the egg for a binder that washes out with water.
