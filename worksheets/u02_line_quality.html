Line Quality

(+ line weight)

Every line says something. Line quality is a line's personality: calm, nervous, loose, sharp. Line weight is how thick or dark it is. Use both on purpose and a flat outline starts to look 3D.

03When to go thick

Outside vs. insidethick outline, thin inside details
Golden rule: Thick lines come forward. Thin lines fall back. Build weight by going back over a line, not by pressing so hard you wreck the tip.
Line quality · Part 2

Line practice

Pen only, no erasing. Use one fineliner for thin lines and a thicker pen (or go back over your line) for heavy lines.

1Line sampler

Copy each line quality from the front. In the last two boxes, invent your own and give each one a name.

2Swell lines

Draw three long curves that go thin, thick, thin. Press a little harder in the middle, or go back over just the middle.

3Add the weight

These are drawn in one thin weight. Trace over them using the four rules. The light comes from the top left.

4Overlap stack

Draw three things stacked in front of each other (fruit, rocks, shoes, anything). Front = thickest line, back = thinnest.
