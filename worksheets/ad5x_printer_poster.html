3D Printing

(Flashforge AD5X)

It melts plastic and stacks it one thin layer at a time, in up to 4 colors. It's hot and open, so read this before your first print.

1How to print
1Get your file

An STL in mm that fits in a 220 mm cube.

2Slice it

Orca-Flashforge: pick AD5X, 0.4 nozzle. Lay it flat. Supports past 45°.

3Check the preview

First layer, time + grams. Show Mr. Lewis before you send it.

4Prep the printer

Plate clean, flat on the magnets, no fingerprints. Right filament loaded.

5Watch it start

Stay for the first 5 minutes. Bad first layer? Stop it early.

6Pop it off

Let it cool, flex the plate. Safety glasses to snip supports.

2Pick your filament

The spool label wins. Keep spools sealed in a bag so they stay dry.

Stay safe
  • Nozzle 300°C, bed 110°C. Hands off while it runs.
  • Hair, hoodie strings + lanyards back.
  • Never reach in mid-print.
  • Never leave it running alone.
  • Weird smell or sound? Stop it, get Mr. Lewis.
4Uh oh
Won't stickwash the plate: dish soap + water. add a brim.
Spaghettistop it. the first layer came loose. clean + retry.
Hairy stringswet filament. dry it, or drop the nozzle 5–10°.
